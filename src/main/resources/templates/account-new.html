Create an ordinary account

Provision a local account, then transfer the initial password securely to the intended user. Their first sign-in requires passkey enrollment and verification before they can change collections or issue client access.

Use 1–64 ASCII letters, digits, dots, underscores or hyphens, starting with a letter or digit. The login identity cannot be edited here.

At least fourteen characters and at most seventy-two UTF-8 bytes. This form cannot create an administrator or configure an external identity mapping.

Organization accounts are provisioned through the operator's configured subject allowlist, or a user's protected identity-linking workflow.
