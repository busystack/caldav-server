Account recovery

Keep recovery codes offline. A code can replace a lost passkey after you recently verify your primary sign-in. It cannot replace your password or upstream sign-in.

Unused codes: · expires .

Generate new recovery codes

Requires recent primary and passkey verification. Generating a new set revokes every unused code from the previous set. Codes appear once.

Recover from a lost passkey

First verify your primary sign-in. Recovery consumes this code, removes all passkeys, revokes DAV and OAuth access, and ends every browser session. Then sign in again and enroll a new passkey.
