Finish setting up your account

  1. Enroll and verify your first passkey. Your primary sign-in and passkey protect account changes.
  2. Create a calendar or task list, or ask an owner to share one with your username.
  3. Issue an application credential for a CalDAV client, unless your application uses registered OAuth authorization.
  4. Save recovery codes offline so a lost passkey does not lock you out.
