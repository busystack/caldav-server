Data preservation and limits

Exports combine resources into one VCALENDAR when their calendar headers and timezone definitions are identical, preserving component bytes. Otherwise, a lossless RFC 5545 stream keeps distinct calendar contexts intact and uses a -stream.ics filename. BusyStack imports both formats. Thunderbird 159.0 requires a single VCALENDAR for file import; verify other applications before migration. Recurrence exceptions stay with their UID series.

Exports are bounded to 10,000 resources and 64 MiB. Larger exports fail before a download starts. Imports preserve existing resources: identical resources are skipped and different resources with an existing UID are reported as conflicts. Valid new series are committed together; rejected, conflicting and skipped items are explicitly listed. A failure while writing rolls back every new resource.

Import previews expire after ten minutes and are replaced after your next upload validates successfully. Restarting this instance invalidates pending previews. Files are stored temporarily on this server, not in browser storage.
